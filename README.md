## Entry Points for Contributors

**Start anywhere. Fork freely. No permission needed.**

### Story Entry Points

- Write a scene where a modern character inherits a Roman household shrine (lararium) and begins experiencing the same cold dread and prophetic dreams as Marcus.
- Create a side-story from the perspective of Aulus, the Greek secretary, documenting the 'case' of the shadow-man with clinical detachment that slowly breaks down.
- Draft a chapter where Livia, the wife, takes the doll to a Vestal Virgin or a philosopher for a second opinion, navigating the different spheres of Roman belief.
- Write a prequel chapter showing Marcus's father, the augur Gaius Fabius Strabo, performing the original ritual that incurred the debt.

### Technical Entry Points

- Build an interactive map of Marcus's Rome, linking locations from the story (his house, the Subura, the Capitoline) with historical notes and textual excerpts.
- Create a 'Codex of Curses' webpage that catalogs the ritual objects, prayers, and omens from the novel, comparing them to historical Roman practices.
